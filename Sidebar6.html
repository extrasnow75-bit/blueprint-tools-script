What Does This Tool Do?

Takes the directions in your model module and uses AI to adapt them to each target module’s own activities. You choose which activities to deploy and which modules receive them, and the AI rewrites the wording to fit each one. Best when modules are similar in shape but need directions tailored to their specific content.

Recommended: run a small test first. Deploy just one activity to two or three target modules, then check the results in the document. Once the wording, headings, and links look right, come back and run the full deployment. This catches problems in about a minute instead of after a long run.

How big a job is reasonable? The work = activities × target modules (“insertions”). On the free Gemini tier, keep it under about 40 for a fast, reliable run; 40–90 still works but slows down; over 90 is best split into a few smaller passes.

Example: in a 15-module course, one module is your model, leaving 14 as targets. Deploying 1 activity → 14 insertions (fast, ~1–2 min). Deploying all 8 activities → 112 insertions (large — deploy in a few smaller passes).
